# Contributor Covenant — Code of Conduct v2.1

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the WhisperFlow
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive: empathy, respect, constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, what's best for the
community.

Unacceptable: sexualized language or attention, trolling/insulting/derogatory comments, personal
or political attacks, harassment, publishing others' private information without consent, or any
conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the maintainers via
[GitHub contact links](https://github.com/namandhakad712/whisperflow). All complaints are
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers who violate this code face the same
consequences as other members.
