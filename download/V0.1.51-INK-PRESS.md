# WhisperFlow — v0.1.51 "The Ink-Press Pass"

## THE BLACK VEIL — root-caused and fixed
Your screenshot showed the whole console reading dark. Pixel forensics
on the shot: **every surface sat at exactly 0.50× its true luminance**
(Lino bg `#FAF6ED` → rgb(124,121,113), white cards → (127,125,122),
accent `#B56A0F` → (93,55,10)) — a mathematically perfect 50% black
blend, clipped exactly to the app window (the desktop and other
windows around it were untouched; the slate band + purple sliver at
the top were other apps peeking behind).

**Root cause**: the paper-grain texture (a `body::before` layer at
opacity 0.5) built its tile with an SVG `feImage` + nested data-URI.
On your Windows Chromium build the filter chain fails, and the
fallback `<rect>` renders its **default black fill** — the whole layer
becomes a half-black veil. My Linux QA renders the grain fine, which
is why it never showed here.

**Fix**: the grain is now plain `<rect>` bayer dots — no filter, no
nested URI, nothing to fail. Same whisper-quiet look. QA pixel check:
Lino bg luminance **250** (was ~124). A troubleshooting entry went
into the README (grain: off was the stopgap).
