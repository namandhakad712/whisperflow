# WhisperFlow — v0.1.53 "Strip Ranges"

## The press runs at three ranges
The halftone day strip (v0.1.52) now zooms — **TODAY / WEEK / MONTH
chips** on the History card:

- **today** — minute buckets, 00–24 ruler, midnight anchor
- **week** — hour buckets, mon–sun ruler, Monday anchor
- **month** — day buckets, 01–30 ruler, the-1st anchor

Each range re-buckets the runs, re-rulers the strip, and re-anchors the
elapsed hairline to the window start. Tooltip formats follow the range
(`14:32` / `tue 09h` / `17 11h`). The entrance press-in re-runs on
range change under motion; instantly under reduced motion.

Verified in the real Electron app with seeded month-long history: all
three ranges bright (lum 244), chips toggle with correct aria-pressed
state, rulers correct (VLM read "THE WEEK, IN INK" + mon–sun, "THE
MONTH, IN INK" + 01–30, dots spread across the full width). Console QA
shots 52-strip-week / 53-strip-month added to the harness.

## MOTION.md — press-kit envelopes
The console's authored motion is now in the grammar, per the queue:
- **Press-in entrance (DayStrip)** — dots scale 0.4 px → radius on
  out-expo, 500 ms, once per mount/range; instant under reduced motion.
- **DotRadar wake** — base 0.8 px dots grow toward 2.6 px within 130 px
  of the cursor, eased by smoothed cursor (lerp 0.14/frame).
- **Spotlight settle** — 240 ms out-expo lift, ≤ 4 px, one shadow step.
- Range changes redraw in place — **the strip is data, not theater.**
