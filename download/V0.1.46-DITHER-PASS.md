# WhisperFlow — v0.1.46 "The Dither Pass"

You sent tripwire.sh/dither-kit; its techniques now live in the app. House
rule held: no third-party component library ships in our UI — the grammar
was studied, the implementation is ours, and INSPIRATION.md records the
lineage (like every technique we've ported).

## What shipped

### Engine F — DitherCanvas (packages/signal-engine)
Ordered Bayer 8×8 dither fills for UI surfaces, zero-dependency Canvas2D:
- **Variants**: `gradient` (dense ink low → airy top), `dotted`, `hatched`
- **Aura bloom**: soft blurred ink underlay beneath the dots
- **Entrance sweep**: 620ms out-expo, dots appear with the wavefront;
  `replayToken` re-runs it
- Discipline intact: IO-gated, DPR capped at 2, reduced-motion → static,
  and dot DENSITY carries tone — every dot stays full ink, which is what
  makes a dither read as print instead of a faded fill

### Insights — data printed as dots
- 7-day words chart: seven dithered bars in the theme accent with aura,
  a **replay** button, and a gliding **scrub tooltip** (hover a bar)
- Stat cards carry a quiet dotted wash
- **Sparkline**: "the week, one line — fig. 02" under the chart
- 12-week heatmap: five dot-density steps of the accent — a press ramp,
  not flat alpha steps

### Overlay system — depth pass
- Gradient hairline ring on the capsule (top-lit, mask-composited) —
  glass over ink, and it fades to nothing in rest state
- **Theme-accent bridge**: the capsule glows in the print you chose —
  phosphor on Lab, print green on Press Room, cobalt on Night Ink…
- Final words land with a 150ms word-in rise; partials stay flat so
  live text never jiggles while you speak

### Bugs found by QA (and fixed)
1. Dots painted gray, not ink — blend math flattened the accent into the
   paper; density now carries tone, color stays full ink
2. Canvases mounted before flex granted height painted once at 78×2px and
   never re-measured — ResizeObserver repaints on layout change
3. Strict-config typecheck errors in the glyph/painted engines
4. My QA history-seed counted words²/30 — bars are now verified to match
   word counts EXACTLY (DOM heights 21/96/51/5/128/69/45 vs expected
   22.4/96/50.7/6.4/128/69.3/46.4 across the seeded week)
