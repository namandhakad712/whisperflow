# WhisperFlow — v0.1.47 "The InkBloom Pass"

You sent two references — a voice-UI showcase (Jakub Antalik's Voice library)
and a generative nocturne (Odyssey's "Somewhere beyond the hills") — and asked
for max creativity, masterpiece, no limits. Here's what shipped, ~90 minutes
later, in the same release discipline as always: house rules held (no copied
values, no installed kits — techniques studied, lineage recorded), everything
built in-house, everything verified.

## The decode

**Voice Glow** (libraries.dev/voice): an audio-reactive particle field that
rises with your voice from an input's bottom edge, sweeps side-to-side while
the reply is "thought through", with emotion detection and hover states —
rendered in glowing RGB pixels on dark glass.

**Odyssey**: tonal nocturnes — velvet dunes, star fields, one glowing
horizon arc, film-grain, dreamlike camera drift.

Our translation: **the voice, printed.** Not RGB confetti — halftone INK.
Not neon glassmorphism — a night landscape in two inks on paper.

## What shipped

### Engine G — InkBloom (packages/signal-engine)
A zero-dep Canvas2D halftone field that rises with the voice along the
capsule's bottom edge:
- **Tide-mark tone**: ink pools at the base, airy at the crest — the ink
  rises like a tide mark, not a flame (took two formula fixes; the QA band
  analysis caught both inversions)
- **Emotion print**: calm / intent / bright / urgent — derived live from
  the level stream's mean + variance; each tunes ink mix, rise height,
  sweep speed, shimmer
- **Thinking sweep**: a press-roller crosses the field left→right while
  the model works
- Bayer-granular crest, ~2Hz idle shimmer, reduced-motion static, DPR≤2

### Overlay
- Bloom strip layers under the glyph wave and live text, over the glass
- Capsule hover: hairline leans into the theme accent, glass lifts, a
  soft accent halo; press sets INTO the sheet (120ms, print discipline)
- Settings cards get the same press state

### Website — the nocturne closes the page
SCENE_NOCTURNE: night dunes with contour striations, a sparse twinkling
star field, one horizon arc that glows in accent ink — two-ink print in
light (night-blue on cream) and dark (pale ink on the deep sheet).
Replaces the re-used orchid at the CTA.

## Bugs the new QA found (and fixed)
1. The overlay returns null while idle → the mount-once effect never saw
   the canvas → **callback-ref attach** (this would have shipped blank!)
2. Depth formula inverted (flame not tide) — twice; band analysis caught it
3. Fake-mic harness: real getUserMedia rejection surfaced as an error-state
   capsule — now a 210Hz oscillator feeds real levels

## Verified
- Bloom painted in all states (listening/processing), 5000+ ink px per shot,
  dot σ56 structure, tide-mark gradient (crest 770 < base 856)
- Nocturne: blue night ink + green accent arc confirmed by pixel audit in
  both modes + VLM visual review — text sits clean on the horizon
- CI 5/5 first try; release live; LATEST FEED = v0.1.47
