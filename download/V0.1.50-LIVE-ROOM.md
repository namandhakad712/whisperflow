# WhisperFlow — v0.1.50 "The Live Room"

You called out: stuck setup checklist, overlay buttons overflowing, "the

PC specs, README, navigation redesign. All of it shipped.

## Bug fixes (root causes found)

### The setup checklist that never finished
Root cause: it had **no completion state at all** — reach 5/5 and the
card just sat there, everything struck-through, "5/5" forever. Also the
"Test your hotkey" step only ticked after your first-ever dictation
(by design — history is the proof), but the hint never said so.

Fix: when all five are done the card **collapses to a single "The press
is ready" strip** with a Dismiss button (remembered in localStorage).
The hotkey step now says what actually ticks it: "Dictate once — the
overlay appears, the step checks itself."

### Buttons overflowing the card
Root cause: the settings `Row` component pinned controls in a
non-wrapping right column — the 7 Position pills needed more room than
they got and blew out the card edge.

Fix: `Row` now **flex-wraps** — wide controls drop below the label
cleanly. Position pills also wrap and gained hover tooltips (their
hints were hidden before).

## The overlay is REAL now (stage v2)
- **Test mic** runs your actual microphone: getUserMedia →
  AnalyserNode → the phosphor trace. Speak and it moves.
- The capsule **mirrors real dictation**: hold your hotkey anywhere
  and the stage follows (listening → printing), with a "live" tag; the
  demo buttons are labeled what they are — a rehearsal.
- The chrome bar shows **your real display**: resolution · scale ·
  label (`2560×1440 · 1.25× · primary display`), and a spec strip
  below the stage shows cpu · ram · gpu · work area · tier. The
  AppHardware IPC was extended to carry primary-display info.

## The sidebar is LIVE now (rail v2)
- **Test the mic** button appears whenever the mic meter sits at its
  floor (permission never granted / no input) — one tap, 10 seconds of
  real meter.
- The **today cell** now counts the whole day — chars plus
  "5 runs · 10s spoken" — recomputed live as history lands (it
  previously showed only the first history line's length).

## Navigation redesigned
- The rail reads in **three mono sections**: THE PRESS (Home,
  Dictation, Models, Personalize) · THE RECORDS (Activity) · THE
  SYSTEM (System).
- **Alt+1…9** jumps straight to a channel — the numbers printed beside
  each icon (renumbered from 00).
- Contrast pass: section headers, channel numbers and the spec line
  brightened after VLM QA flagged them faint.
