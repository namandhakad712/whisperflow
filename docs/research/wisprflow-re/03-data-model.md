## 3. Learning-loop columns
After each dictation the app observes the target text field: `textboxContents` → `editedTextStatus`/`editedTextAttempts` extraction → `pastedText`, `formattingDivergenceScore` (20240827), `editDistanceToDictated` + `editedTextUnbounded` (20260717/20), `contentObservationEndReason`/`LastKeystroke`, `hasRevertedAI` — measuring whether AI formatting survived. Quality signals: `averageLogProb`, `toneMatchedText`/`toneMatchPairs`, `numWordsCorrected`/`numDictionaryReplacements` (ML-server stats), `feedback` + `desiredAsr`/`desiredFormatted` (gold corrections). Provider experiments: default-vs-fallback texts/scores, `usedFallback*`, `fallbackLevel`, `clientNetworkLatency`, `calledExternalAsr`/`transcriptOrigin`. Everything carries `needsUploading` and ends up in UsageOutbox (content-free) and Polish/InstructHistory metrics.

## 4. Retention
Two knobs: **(a) Dictations** — Settings → "Auto-delete local data every 24hrs" = `localDataPolicy` {store_normally, delete_after_24h, never_store, always_store}; org settings can force stricter. Confirm copy: *"This will delete transcripts and polish history older than 24 hours across all your devices"* → **History + Polish** older than 24h (History holds the audio/screenshot BLOBs); never-store stops capture and shows "History disabled". **(b) Meeting transcripts** — `notetakerTranscriptRetention` {never_delete, 1…365 days}, admin-set: *"Transcripts older than {{duration}} will be deleted within 24 hours"*; `Meetings.transcriptDeletedAt` marks transcript-only deletion (notes survive).
